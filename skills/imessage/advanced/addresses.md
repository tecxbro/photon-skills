# Advanced iMessage addresses

- Use the documented address resource to validate phone numbers and email addresses and determine iMessage availability.
- Normalize only with official helpers; do not infer availability from E.164 shape or email syntax.
- Treat unavailable, malformed, and transient lookup failures as distinct outcomes.
- Do not use address checks to enumerate or profile people without a legitimate application need.

Official source: <https://photon.codes/docs/advanced-kits/imessage/addresses>
