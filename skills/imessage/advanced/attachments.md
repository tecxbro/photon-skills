# Advanced iMessage attachments

- Upload a file, inspect metadata, send by uploaded reference, and stream downloads.
- Prefer streams for large attachments and enforce application-level size limits.
- Do not assume an attachment has a public URL.
- Validate MIME type and filename separately from content.
- For HEIC/HEIF conversion, route to [`../../heif2jpeg/SKILL.md`](../../heif2jpeg/SKILL.md).

Official source: <https://photon.codes/docs/advanced-kits/imessage/attachments>
