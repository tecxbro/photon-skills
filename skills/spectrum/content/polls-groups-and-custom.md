# Polls, groups, and custom content

- Use poll and option builders for structured questions and inbound selections.
- Use visual groups to bundle related messages without confusing them with a group chat.
- Respect nesting restrictions.
- Use custom content only after narrowing to the intended provider.
- Validate raw provider payloads and keep generic agent logic independent of them.
