# iMessage deliverability

- Follow current official deliverability guidance.
- Use consent-based, expected messaging flows.
- Avoid bursty or repetitive patterns that resemble abuse.
- Respect opt-out and conversation context.
- Do not invent undocumented daily limits or claim a fixed safe sending rate.

Official source: <https://photon.codes/docs/best-practices/imessage-deliverability>
